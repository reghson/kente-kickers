Kente Kickers: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Kente Kickers web game (reghson.github.io/kente-kickers) and the Kente Kickers Android app (com.reghson.kentekickers).

Short version

Kente Kickers itself does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no analytics, no accounts and no sign-in. The website never shows ads. The Android app may show optional ads from Google AdMob in a future update (see "Advertising" below); the current version shows none.

What stays on your device

Your club, players, settings and progress are saved only on your own device (browser local storage or the app's local storage). This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it.

Advertising (Android app only)

The website has no ads and no advertising code. The Android app includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, which is switched off in the current version: it is not started and no ads are requested. If ads are turned on in a later update:

  • Ads appear only outside matches: at most one full-screen ad between matches (no more than once every 3 matches), and an optional ad you can choose to watch after a match for bonus coins. There are no ads during gameplay.
  • Before any ad is loaded in regions that require it (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • Google AdMob may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This is done by Google under the "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" page and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Ads are requested with a "G" (general audiences) content rating.

The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Network use

The web version downloads the game files from GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement. The Android app bundles the game files and plays fully offline.

In-app purchases (Android app only)

The Android app may offer optional packs of in-game Coins and Gold Kente. The same currency can always be earned for free by playing. Purchases are never offered on the website. If you buy a pack, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your card or payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can add the Coins or Gold to the save on your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: open an issue at github.com/reghson/kente-kickers/issues.
