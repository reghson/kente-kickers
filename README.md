# ⚽ Kente Kickers

An original, free football game for your phone: build your own club, manage the squad and play real-time matches.

**Play:** https://reghson.github.io/kente-kickers/ (install it from the browser menu with "Add to Home screen". It works offline after the first load.)

## Features
- **Build your club:** name, short name, crest (colours, pattern, badge) and home kit, plus 16 made-up players with Ghanaian and West African names.
- **Manage:** 5 formations (4-4-2, 4-3-3, 3-5-2, 4-2-3-1, 5-3-2), pick your XI, train players with coins, buy and sell on the transfer market.
- **Play:** top-down real-time matches (2, 3 or 5 minutes) with a touch joystick and Pass, Shoot (hold for power) and Tackle/Sprint buttons. AI teammates and opponents, goalkeepers, corners, goal kicks and throw-ins. Up to 3 substitutions. Quick sim if you are in a hurry.
- **Career:** six divisions, 8 clubs each, a home-and-away season, promotion and relegation, prize money, stadium upgrades and a trophy cabinet.
- Sound effects made in the browser, with mute. Progress saves on your device.

## Controls
| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Drag on left half of screen | Arrows / WASD |
| Pass / switch player | PASS | J or Space |
| Shoot (hold = power) | SHOOT | K or Enter |
| Tackle (near opponent) / Sprint (hold) | TACKLE | L or Shift |
| Pause, subs, sim | ⏸ | P or Esc |

## Notes
All clubs, players, crests and names are fictional. No ads, no payments, no tracking. This is a plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript PWA with no build step.
